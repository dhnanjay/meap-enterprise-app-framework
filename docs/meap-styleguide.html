5 · Controls and feedback

One primary action per region. Destructive actions are outlines. Errors identify the failure and the next action.

3 exceptions need an explanation. The reconciliation cannot be submitted until each has a disposition.
The source workbook is missing a required column. Add value_date and upload again. ERR-39218

Do

  • Use amber for unexplained differences.
  • Use mono for support identifiers.
  • Leave most of the screen neutral.

Don't

  • Use red for normal accounting findings.
  • Use blue on inert labels.
  • Add a sixth status color.

6 · Writing and rules

AvoidWriteReason
Good to see you.WorkspacesName the work surface.
SubmitSubmit for reviewState the outcome.
Total exceptionsOpen exceptionsName the actionable set.
DifferenceUnexplained differenceName the accounting meaning.
Something went wrong.The source workbook is missing a required column.Make support possible.

Non-negotiable

  • Three page archetypes.
  • 36/44px density only.
  • Tabular, right-aligned figures.
  • Visible focus and native fallback.

Out of scope in v1

  • Dark mode.
  • A third density.
  • Decorative animation.
  • A general icon set.
